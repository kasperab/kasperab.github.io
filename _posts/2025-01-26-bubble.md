With the theme of the jam being "bubble", we had a lot of small ideas of what we could do with that. After some discussion we ended up deciding to put some of these small ideas into a WarioWare-style collection of mingames. This allowed us to more easily manage our time, by simply making small games until we couldn't make more. We also decided to make the games be two players versus each other, which we thought would make it more fun.

I worked on the game where you pop the bubble with a needle, and the game where you blow bubblegum until it bursts. My main challenge was implementing somewhat vague gameplay, but I managed to finish both with some time to spare (though not enough time to make another game). With some nice graphics and sounds, I think it turned into a fun little game.
